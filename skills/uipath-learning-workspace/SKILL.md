# UiPath Learning Workspace

Build durable capability rather than temporary fluency by connecting current primary-source knowledge to small practical wins, effortful retrieval, spaced review, and real-world demonstration.

**Maturity:** pilot.

## Ownership Boundary

**This custom skill owns:** Build durable capability rather than temporary fluency by connecting current primary-source knowledge to small practical wins, effortful retrieval, spaced review, and real-world demonstration.

It does not own current UiPath product commands, schemas, artifact validation, live tenant operations, or policy administration unless its instructions explicitly say otherwise.

## Workflow

### 1. Define the mission

Record why the learner needs the topic, target work situations, success demonstrations, constraints, timeline, and preferred teaching style.

**Completion criterion:** MISSION.md describes a real outcome rather than a broad topic.

### 2. Assess the starting point

Use a short evidence-based baseline: explanation, artifact review, diagnostic task, or small build. Record existing strengths and misconceptions without turning assessment into a long exam.

**Completion criterion:** The next lesson sits just beyond current capability.

### 3. Curate current sources

Use official UiPath documentation, official skills and repositories, product release information, specifications, and carefully selected practitioner resources. Record freshness and purpose.

**Completion criterion:** Every lesson can point to a high-trust primary source.

### 4. Create one short lesson

Teach only the knowledge needed for one tangible skill, include a worked example or visual model, and keep the lesson small enough to complete in one focused sitting.

**Completion criterion:** The learner can demonstrate one new capability at the end.

### 5. Add retrieval and feedback

Use recall questions, prediction, debugging, modification, comparison, or a small lab with immediate feedback. Avoid answer-format clues.

**Completion criterion:** Practice requires active retrieval or performance, not rereading.

### 6. Record and space learning

Write a learning record with demonstrated capability, errors, corrections, confidence, and the next review date. Interleave related topics when appropriate.

**Completion criterion:** Progress and future review are durable across sessions.

### 7. Advance through real work

Select the next lesson from the mission and learning records, and periodically require a project, review, or explanation that transfers beyond the lesson environment.

**Completion criterion:** The curriculum adapts to demonstrated performance.

## Output Contract

- MISSION.md.
- Curated RESOURCES.md.
- Short lessons and reference notes.
- Practice artifacts and feedback.
- Learning records and spaced-review plan.

## Guardrails

- Do not rely on memory for current UiPath product facts.
- Do not confuse familiarity with demonstrated skill.
- Do not overload one lesson with an entire product.
- Do not continue a fixed curriculum when evidence shows a different next step.

## Example Requests

- "Teach my team UiPath agents over six weeks."
- "Create a learning path from XAML to coded automation."
- "Help a solution architect learn Maestro through real projects."

## Finish

End with what was completed, the evidence produced, the next official skill or owner, and every unresolved blocker. Never imply that a write, validation, test, deployment, policy change, tenant operation, or runtime action occurred unless it actually ran and its result was observed.
