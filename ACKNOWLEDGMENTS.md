# Acknowledgments

UiPath Boost is an independent community project created by
[1aifanatic](https://github.com/1aifanatic). It is not an official UiPath
product or skill catalog.

The catalog was inspired in part by the composable engineering-skill patterns
published in [Matt Pocock's skills repository](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills),
including focused skill ownership, user-invoked and model-invoked workflows,
decision frontiers, leading words, progressive disclosure, and checkable
completion criteria. Those ideas have been adapted for UiPath delivery rather
than copied as a replacement for the original general engineering skills.

Matt Pocock's repository is distributed under the
[MIT License](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/blob/main/LICENSE). UiPath
Boost retains this acknowledgment so the source of those design influences is
clear.

Current UiPath product commands, schemas, artifact validation, deployment,
tenant operations, and platform behavior remain owned by the
[official UiPath skills repository](https://github.com/UiPath/skills).
